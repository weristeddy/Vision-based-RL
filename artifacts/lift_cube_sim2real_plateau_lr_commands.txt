Sim2Real Lift-Cube -- FULL SUBMIT COMMANDS, plateau-corrected learning rates
==============================================================================

Supersedes the learning rates in lift_cube_sim2real_submit_commands.txt.
Everything else about those commands -- camera, task IDs, profile, the rest of
the hyperparameters -- is unchanged.

Why the learning rates move. The retained runs all used schedule=adaptive, so
the rate in their run config is a starting point, not the rate they trained at.
Every one of them sits in the same reach-but-never-lift plateau -- `lift` near
1.1, `lift_precise` exactly 0, cube height 0.019 -- and then escapes. The rate
that matters is the median while still in the plateau, read out of
Loss/learning_rate in each retained run's own history:

The two that failed on the new camera are the two extremes: NatureCnn-
SpatialSoftmax needed 10.57x its configured rate and R3M-SpatialSoftmax 2.59x,
and both were re-run at 1.00x. DinoV2-SpatialSoftmax needed 0.88x, was re-run
at 1.32x, and worked -- which is why it was the one unaffected encoder.

schedule=fixed everywhere below, deliberately. Adaptive ramps to its 1.0e-02
ceiling in the first iteration -- a fresh policy makes tiny updates, KL comes
out near zero, and the rule multiplies by 1.5 per minibatch -- and whether it
recovers is luck. The retained R3M run recovered to 1.30e-03; the new one took
a KL spike to 0.64 at iteration 2,000 and was driven to the 1.0e-05 floor for
its last 4,000 iterations. Fixing the rate removes that coin flip. The cost is
that a fixed rate cannot back off, so watch Loss/approx_kl against each job's
desired-kl in the first few hundred iterations.

The DinoV2 rows could equally keep --agent.algorithm.schedule adaptive, since
that is proven on the new camera for DinoV2-SpatialSoftmax. Fixed is used here
only for consistency.

CAMERA -- verified, not assumed. All twelve Sim2Real task IDs share one camera
setup: sensor `cam`, 224x224, enabled_geom_groups (0, 2). All twelve CollisionCam
IDs use (0, 3). Checked by loading every registered config; one distinct setup
per generation, no per-architecture variation.

What (0, 2) buys, from commit fb67b28:
  * the visual meshes instead of the collision proxies the retained runs saw
  * group 1 excluded, and fb67b28 moved camera_visual, appearance_camera_face
    and the four lens/optic geoms into group 1 -- an external camera still sees
    the D405 body, the wrist imager behind it does not. The commit message notes
    MuJoCo was drawing camera_glass as an opaque surface 4 mm from the imager,
    which blacked out the wrist view.

The FOV is part of this too, and reading the XML alone hides it: both XMLs still
say fovy="87" on robot/cam, but wxai_constants.py overrides it at build time with
_D405_CROP_VERTICAL_FOV_DEG = 54.49, which tasks/utils/camera.py passes into
CameraSensorCfg. Resolving every registered config gives fovy=54.49. That is the
D405's measured 224-crop vertical FOV; the old 87 was its horizontal FOV in the
vertical field, which rendered every object 1.72x too small.

The override is unconditional, so the CollisionCam IDs render 54.49 now as well.
Two consequences worth keeping straight:
  * The retained numbers in the last column were measured at fovy 87. Comparing
    a new run against them mixes the FOV change with the geom-group change.
  * The r3m_old / r3m_new probe comparison in
    artifacts/lift_cube_sim2real_results.txt therefore isolated geom groups
    only, both at 54.49 -- not "old camera vs new camera" as that section says.

Run from the repo root on the cluster login node. Independent; submit any subset.
