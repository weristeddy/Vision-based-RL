Push-T VisualGoal rerun with colour DR -- the five architectures that learned
=============================================================================

What changed in the task since the runs below were measured:

  - the T object's colour is resampled uniform over the RGB cube every reset
    (scene real_texture_red -> real_texture, which restores object_color)
  - the goal marker's colour is resampled the same way, independently
  - the marker is still the filled T, unchanged

The two colour draws are unguarded, so on ~0.4% of resets they land within an
RGB distance of 0.1 and on ~22% within a 1.2:1 luminance ratio. Because the
marker is filled it shares the object's silhouette exactly, so those episodes
present two near-identical shapes. That is deliberate.

Everything else is untouched: sampling, reward, threshold, camera.

  object_pose_range      x (0.20, 0.40)  y (-0.20, 0.20)  z 0.013  yaw (-pi, pi)
  target_position_range  x (0.30, 0.50)  y (-0.20, 0.20)  z 0.012
  target_yaw_range       (-pi, pi)       min_xy_separation 0.15 m
  success_threshold      0.90            episode 5.0 s

Hyperparameters. All 24 original VisualGoal runs shared one set, and it already
matches the registered rl_cfg except for two values, which is why they are the
only flags here:

  gamma           registered 0.99   the runs used 0.996
  max_iterations  registered 3000   the runs used 6000

Everything else -- lr 2e-4, schedule fixed, desired_kl 0.05, entropy_coef
0.001, 8 epochs x 16 minibatches, max_grad_norm 0.5, accumulation 8,
num_steps_per_env 16, 1024 envs per rank -- comes from the registered config
and needs no flag.

The baseline these five are being compared against:

  DinoV2ViTS14-Afa6             at_goal 0.709   62e4elj6
  NatureCnn-SpatialSoftmax      at_goal 0.553   00zl7414
  DinoV2ViTS14-LocalGrid16      at_goal 0.160   thoas0n9
  DinoV2ViTS14-SpatialSoftmax   at_goal 0.028   1b5jdi3y
  R3MResNet50-Linear            at_goal 0.013   iq1clp9p

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All five in one go. --arch filters submission only; the other ten VisualGoal
IDs stay registered so their runs remain loadable.

bash cluster/submit_sweep.sh --variant VisualGoal --profile b200 \
  --arch "DinoV2ViTS14-Afa6,NatureCnn-SpatialSoftmax,DinoV2ViTS14-LocalGrid16,DinoV2ViTS14-SpatialSoftmax,R3MResNet50-Linear" \
  -- --agent.algorithm.gamma 0.996 --agent.max-iterations 6000

Add --dry-run first to see the five job lines without submitting.
