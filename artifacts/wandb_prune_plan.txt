W&B STORAGE PRUNE PLAN
====================================================================================================
Surveyed 2192 runs across 4 projects: 174.46 GB of run-file storage.
Artifact storage is separate and already clean -- 3.19 GB, 26 model artifacts.
Nothing has been deleted. Every number below is measured, not estimated.

Protected from every tier: the 16 runs referenced by the repo
(ckpts/lift_cube/provenance.json, configs/analysis/*, export_onnx.py, the two
resume sources in artifacts/push_t_orientation_batch.txt). Their final
checkpoint is kept. Note the 12 lift-cube retained policies also exist on disk
in ckpts/lift_cube/ (1.28 GB), so W&B is provenance for them, not the only copy.

HOW IT RUNS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
prune.py reads delete_candidates.json and calls File.delete() per file, with
--dry-run by default, --tier {1,2} to select, and a per-category breakdown of what
it actually removed. It never touches a run object, so no curve is deleted.
